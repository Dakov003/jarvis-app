# Jarvis — Privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

- It reads the owner's Google Calendar and writes only to a calendar it created, to send reminders to the owner.
- Data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
